Verifies and diagnoses a local Electron app at runtime by attaching to the already running dev app, with main-process, renderer and utility-process logs, code evaluation, screenshots and accessibility snapshots. Use when the user changed or asks to check Electron main-process, BrowserWindow, preload, contextBridge or IPC code, or reports a blank window, crash or runtime error in an Electron app, including requests like “验证应用还能不能正常工作”, “改了 preload / IPC 帮我验证”, “白屏”, “崩溃”, “运行时报错”, even when the project has its own tests. Not for packaged apps or pure type and build errors.
